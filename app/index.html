Terrain (elevation model)

Samples a DEM across the search area, computes hiking travel time from the IPP with the Tobler function (slope-dependent, uphill slower than gentle downhill), and scores each segment by terrain-adjusted distance.

At 100 cells and a 4 km radius the DEM spacing is about 80 m. More cells means finer terrain and more requests.

Survivability over time

Survival is a time-decay fit by subject category and does not account for weather, injury or clothing. Treat it as one input to urgency, not a limit on effort.
