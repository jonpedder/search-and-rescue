Workflow

  1. Load or name the incident workspace and set the operational period.
  2. Bring Planning segments forward from ArcGIS or add areas and segments with ArcGIS Sketch.
  3. Select map objects from the map, object list or right-side Operational Layers panel; edit attributes or geometry and save.
  4. Create assignments from selected segments, then record team, communications, POD, prior effort and safety details.
  5. Map PLS/LKP information, clues, cell pings, hazards, routes, tracks, facilities and assets.
  6. Use layer and feature Zoom controls plus Arcade popups to review the common operating picture.
  7. Sync unsaved records, export a protected backup and print assignment cards.

Operational data may contain sensitive subject, responder and investigative information. Keep the ArcGIS service private or group-restricted.
