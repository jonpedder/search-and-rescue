Workflow

  1. Load or name the incident workspace.
  2. Bring planning segments forward from ArcGIS or draw operational areas.
  3. Create assignments linked to segments.
  4. Map PLS/LKP information, clues, cell pings and assets.
  5. Sync unsaved records, export a protected backup and print assignment cards.

Operational data may contain sensitive subject, responder and investigative information. Keep the ArcGIS service private or group-restricted.
